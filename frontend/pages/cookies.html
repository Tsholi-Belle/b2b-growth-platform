Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last Updated: August 17, 2026 | Effective Date: August 17, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Amblysomus Solutions uses cookies and browser local storage to provide a seamless, secure user experience.

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies and browser local storage are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. Amblysomus uses them strictly for session management, currency preference selection, and application security.

2. Cookies and Storage Keys We Use

Key / Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
auth_session_token Essential / Security Maintains secure authenticated session and Google MFA status. Session / 24 Hours
userCurrency Preference Remembers user's selected display currency (e.g. ZAR, USD, EUR). 1 Year
demo_mode Functionality Tracks non-authenticated demo evaluation state. Session
clarificationState Functionality Caches Proactive Co-Creator resolved chips across tabs. Session

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers

Amblysomus Solutions does not use third-party behavioral advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or cross-site data brokers. All data remains strictly within our secure South Africa cloud infrastructure.

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings or by clicking "Delete Account / Clear Data" in the Governance tab.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our cookie practices, please contact hello@kalixara.com.

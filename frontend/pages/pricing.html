Frequently Asked Questions

How does annual billing saving work?
When you choose annual billing, you receive a 20% discount on every month. For example, the Starter plan drops from R890/mo to R712/mo (or $49/mo to $39/mo), saving you thousands of Rands each year.
What's included in the 7-day free trial?
You get 7 full days of access to the Professional plan, allowing you to generate unlimited proposals, run cloud stack optimizations, and test all premium features with zero commitment.
Can I cancel or change plans anytime?
Yes! There are no long-term locks for monthly plans. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time via your self-service billing portal.
Do you support South African Rand (ZAR) & Instant EFT?
Yes! Amblysomus natively supports ZAR billing via PayFast (Capitec Pay, Instant EFT, Debit/Credit Card) and international credit cards.

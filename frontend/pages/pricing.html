Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the free trial?
You get 7 days of full access to the Professional plan, allowing you to generate unlimited proposals and test all premium features.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, there are no long-term contracts for Monthly plans. You can cancel at any time and retain access until the end of your billing cycle.
Do you support my currency?
Yes! While base prices are in USD, we support localized checkout and display in EUR, GBP, ZAR, AUD, CAD, INR, NGN, KES, and more.
What happens to my proposals if I cancel?
Your data remains safe. You'll be downgraded to the Free tier, and can still access and export your past proposals.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade encryption and never use your proprietary data to train our foundational models without explicit consent.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits/startups?
Yes! Reach out to sales@archengine.ai to see if you qualify for our 50% impact discount.
